Two Brothers. One Big Idea. Zero Nap Schedules Followed.

Say hello to Anwit, Big Boss & Chief Coloring Officer (age 7, expertise: staying inside the lines most of the time), and Arnit, Little Boss & Chief Tasting Officer (age 8 months, expertise: quality-testing every product by putting it directly in his mouth).

Together, they're known around the house as Team WitNit — and together, they run Pitaraa, proof that you don't need a boardroom (or a driver's license, or the ability to say full sentences) to build something great. A blanket on the floor works just fine.

🧑‍💼 The Big Boss — Anwit, Age 7

Anwit's official title is Founder. His actual job most days is deciding which cartoon character gets colored first, negotiating screen time with mom, and occasionally signing off on very important business decisions like adding glitter to the packaging.

He's the reason every Pitaraa kit is designed the way he'd actually want to color it — bold outlines, characters kids recognize, and pieces sized just right for small hands.

👶 The Little Boss — Arnit, Age 8 Months

Arnit joined the company before he could walk, which honestly makes him one of the youngest founders in Jaipur — maybe India. His contributions include drooling on the first prototypes, giving zero feedback that wasn't a giggle or a cry, and being the unofficial mascot every customer secretly wants to squish.

💛 Why It Matters to You

We know what it's like when your little one won't sit still, when the older one wants big-kid activities, and when you just want twenty mess-free minutes where everyone's actually happy at the same time.

Pitaraa was born out of that exact chaos — one house, two brothers, and a mom and dad watching them create something together instead of fighting over the remote. If it worked for our family, we're pretty confident it'll work for yours too.

So whether you've got your own Anwit, your own Arnit, or one of each — pull up a blanket on the floor. That's really all it takes.
